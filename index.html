From a reference image

Use a picture from a paper, another icon set, ChatGPT or a sketch as a reference. Claude draws a new, original icon in our style.

New illustration from chosen icons
  1. Drag the icons that are the starting point onto the canvas, e.g. a brain, a syringe and a virus.
  2. Select them and group them (Ctrl+G).
  3. Describe the illustration you want, then press Compose.

Nothing to combine yet: put the icons on the canvas, select them and group them (Ctrl+G).

Claude makes a new illustration from them: complex icons such as organs stay exactly as they are, others are redrawn in the same style with the changes needed (e.g. a longer needle, the right liquid, a virus label), and markers or glows are added. Selecting the group also shows this as “AI draw” in the right panel. Ctrl+Z undoes it.

Each request uses your own Claude usage. The first time, Claude asks you to allow this page.
